### Phase 7A: Saved-search alerts and the "Findora picks" widget
- **Saved searches**: "Save search" on Home whenever a filter, category or search text narrows the feed, and "Save this search" under Ask Findora's understood chips (saves, then applies). A name dialog suggests one from the filter ("Kakkanad · Apartment · 2+ bedrooms"); names are trimmed and capped at 60 characters. A third **Searches** list in the Saved tab shows each search (name, sentence and region) with Show results (applies its region and filter to Home), rename, delete, a new-match alert switch and a Daily / On every refresh frequency. Searches are per user (guests included) and deleted with the account or the guest session. `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` is asked when a search is saved on Android 13+; saving works either way.
- **Domain** (`core/domain/alert`, pure Kotlin): `AlertDiff` (new matches = listings that pass the filter and are not in the search's seen set, newest first; daily searches due once a day with one hour of slack, instant ones on every run, refresh runs only check instant searches; price drops against the last rent seen for each favourite), `SaveSearch` (remembers the listings that already match, so only later homes alert) and `CheckSearchAlerts` (checks due searches against the cached listings, records seen ids and baselines, so running it twice never alerts twice).
- **Data**: Room schema **v4** by auto-migration from v3: `saved_searches` gains `region_id`, `alert_frequency` (default daily) and `last_checked_at`; new `saved_search_seen` (search id, listing id) and `price_watch` (user, listing, last rent) tables; `notifications` gains `saved_search_id` and `item_count`. `RoomSavedSearchRepository` (owner-scoped reads and writes, filter stored as versioned JSON by `SavedFilterJson`, which drops unknown values instead of failing) and `RoomPriceWatchRepository`; account and guest deletion clear seen ids and price baselines.
- **A market that moves** (`FakeMarket`, deterministic): from the seed date (2026-10-22) each region publishes two new units a day at 09:00 and 13:00 UTC (a new unit of an existing seed home: same building, photos, agent and features, its own id, unit name, publication time and a rent within ±10%, history scaled alike), listed for 7 days and then let; and every week about one listing in eight is offered 5% cheaper for that week. Everything is a pure function of the listing ids and the clock, so saved-search and price-drop alerts have real events to report without a server, and detail pages resolve the new ids like any other listing.
- **Background work**: `SearchAlertWorker` (`@HiltWorker`) runs every 4 hours (unique periodic work, battery-not-low constraint, no network constraint because the backend is local), refreshes the regions the searches use and checks daily and instant searches plus favourite price drops; a successful listings refresh (`ListingsRefreshListener`, called by `OfflineFirstPropertyRepository`) queues a one-off check of instant searches. It only runs for a signed-in user. New matches and price drops go to the notifications center (in-app toggle) and to a new **Search alerts** channel (new toggle in Settings › Notifications; one notification per search). A new-match notification opens `findora://search/{id}` (manifest filter, `SearchDeepLink`, `SavedSearchResults` route) through the session guard, which applies the search to Home for its owner only; a price drop opens the listing. The notifications center shows both kinds (plural-aware titles, trending-down and bookmark icons) and opens the same targets.
- **Home-screen widget** (Jetpack Glance, `FindoraPicksWidget`): "Findora picks" shows the signed-in user's top three homes (best matches with their score when a preference profile exists, else saved homes, else Recommended) with a downscaled photo (Coil, software bitmaps at 168 px, 5 s timeout, house icon fallback), the price in the display currency and the next upcoming viewing. Tapping a home opens `findora://property/{id}`; tapping elsewhere opens the app; a signed-out widget asks to sign in. Light and dark colours, texts in the Settings language, one TalkBack sentence per home. Refreshed by `PicksWidgetWorker` every 3 hours while a widget is placed (scheduled on the first, cancelled with the last) and a few seconds after favourites or viewings change. Picker preview: a scalable `previewLayout` on Android 12+ (with target cell size) and a vector `previewImage` below.

## [0.6.0] - 2026-10-23 Phase 5: Best match, Dream Match, Compare and natural-language search

### Phase 5C: Ask Findora natural-language search
- **Domain** (`core/domain/search`, pure Kotlin): `NaturalLanguageQueryParser` turns an English, French or Arabic sentence into a `ParsedIntent`: the `PropertyFilter` it describes, every recognised span (`RecognizedToken` with start/end into the original text and its `QueryMeaning`), the words it did not understand, the region it points at (named place or region, else the one region priced in the budget's currency), lifestyle hints and an explicit sort. Deterministic, rule-based and offline: a tokenizer (`QueryText`: Arabic-Indic and Persian digits, thousands groups, decimals, `k` / lakh / mille / ألف multipliers, spelled-out counts, accent and Arabic letter-variant folding, Arabic clitic and article stripping) and grammar rules over a trilingual lexicon (`QueryLexicon`): budgets, ranges, bedrooms, bathrooms, French T/F and "pièces", areas (m², sq ft), types, must-haves, amenities, lifestyle and sort words, currencies. `PlaceIndex` matches the region packs' neighbourhoods and regions exactly (canonical names, comma prefixes, article-less forms, Arabic and common aliases) or with a bounded typo on canonical names. Negations, unsupported bounds and contradictions are reported, never applied.
- **Ask Findora** mode on the Search screen (Places / Ask Findora switch): removable chips for what was understood, "Not understood" words, notes for a region switch, a currency the region does not use, the order that will be used and the quiz when lifestyle words need a profile; "Show homes" replaces the Home filter through `HomeFilterRepository`, switches region when needed, records the sentence in recent searches and sorts by Best match when a profile exists (an explicit "cheapest" / "newest" / "top rated" wins). Nothing understood: an explanation and "Search these words instead". Example prompts per language. Labelled in the UI as on-device, rule-based understanding.
- **Voice input** through the system recognizer (`RecognizerIntent.ACTION_RECOGNIZE_SPEECH`, app language), shown only when a recognizer is installed (`<queries>` in the manifest); no microphone permission.
- English, French and Arabic strings, RTL, light and dark, TalkBack "Remove …" labels on chips, 48 dp targets.
- Tests: `NaturalLanguageQueryParserTest` (table of EN/FR/AR cases, edge cases and no-false-positive checks, spans, removal, determinism, example prompts), new `SearchViewModelTest` cases (Turbine), `AskFindoraScreenTest` (Compose, Robolectric: chips appear, remove and apply).

### Phase 5A: Preference quiz, Best Match engine and explainable match UI
- **Domain** (`core/domain/match`, pure Kotlin): `PreferenceProfile` (budget range with currency, minimum bedrooms and bathrooms, property types, neighbourhood and commute anchors, lifestyle tags quiet / family / pet owner / remote worker / student / nightlife / nature, must-haves parking / furnished / balcony / verified). `MatchScoreCalculator` returns a deterministic 0-100 `MatchResult(score, reasons)` from seven weighted factors: budget fit 30, location or commute 20, size, beds and baths 15, amenities and must-haves 15, style and lifestyle 10, trust and freshness 5, learned behaviour 5. Every factor yields one `Reason` (label key, positive or negative, points). A failed must-have sets the score to 0 and excludes the listing from Best match (it stays visible in normal browsing). An unanswered quiz step never penalises a listing; a budget in another currency or anchors in another region are scored as neutral.
- **Learned behaviour**: `BehaviorAffinity` weights the recorded interaction events (view plus dwell bonus, favourite, unfavourite, share, contact agent, schedule viewing, dismiss) by `0.5^(age / 14 days)` from an injected clock and transfers them to listings sharing the home itself, its type, neighbourhood or bedroom count.
- **Storage**: one versioned JSON document per user in the existing Preferences DataStore (`DataStoreMatchProfileRepository`), not Room: the profile is small, read and written whole and never queried, so no schema bump or migration was needed (Room stays at v2). Unreadable documents read as "no profile"; unknown ids from a newer app are dropped. Deleting an account clears its profile.
- **Quiz** (`feature/match/quiz`, `MatchQuiz` route): six steps (budget range slider bounded by the region's rents, beds and baths, home types, preferred neighbourhoods and a commute destination, lifestyle, must-haves), an animated progress bar announced as "Step n of 6", steps sliding in the reading direction, Back (and system back), Skip (clears that step) and "See my matches", which saves the profile. Opened from a "Find my best match" banner on Home while there is no profile, and from Settings > Match preferences to edit the saved answers.
- **UI touchpoints**: "92% match" glass badge on Home cards only when a profile exists (missing-must-have homes get none) and included in the card's TalkBack summary; a new "Best match" sort in the filter sheet (offered with a profile) that ranks the region's listings by score in memory and leaves out must-have failures, while the previous default becomes "Recommended"; an animated match ring with a count-up and the top three reason chips on the detail page; a "Why this match?" bottom sheet with one staggered animated bar and points per factor. All motion is skipped when system animations are off.
- English, French and Arabic strings, RTL-aware step transitions, light and dark.
- Tests: `MatchScoreCalculatorTest` (property-based style over thousands of generated listings and profiles: score within 0..100 and equal to the rounded reason sum, cheaper within budget never lowers budget fit or the score, a missing must-have always scores 0 and is excluded, decay shrinks old signals, determinism, worked examples), `BestMatchRankingTest`, `DataStoreMatchProfileRepositoryTest` (round trip, per user, restart, corrupt and future data), `MatchQuizViewModelTest` (Turbine), `MatchQuizScreenTest` and `WhyThisMatchTest` (Compose, Robolectric, including dark RTL), new Home, Detail and Settings ViewModel and Home screen cases.
